# OSINT-Tools-Norway
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d9/Flag_of_Norway.svg" alt="Norway_FLAG"/>
A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Norway. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Norwegian (Bokmål and Nynorsk), many also in English. Norway is unusually open: the tax lists (income, wealth and tax of every taxpayer) are searchable, the shareholder register is open to anyone on request, and vehicle and aircraft owners can be looked up. Opsec warning: tax-list searches require ID-porten login and are logged, and the person you look up can see your name, birth year and postcode. Many searches (tax lists, vehicle owner, health personnel register) require Norwegian electronic ID (BankID, MinID). The beneficial-owner register opened on 1 October 2024 but is open only to authorities, obliged entities, media, civil-society organisations and universities. Enter names with Norwegian letters (æ, ø, å).

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [data.norge.no](https://data.norge.no/nb) — Norway's official portal for datasets, APIs and concepts from over 125 public and private organisations, with AI-assisted search. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [Statistikkbanken (Statistics Norway — StatBank)](https://www.ssb.no/statbank/) — Statistics Norway's database of official statistical tables, with downloads and an API. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Norges Bank — Statistikk (Central Bank of Norway — Statistics)](https://www.norges-bank.no/tema/Statistikk/) — interest rates, exchange rates, securities, balance of payments and financial-sector statistics. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Lovdata](https://lovdata.no/) — laws and regulations in force free of charge; the full case-law database is a paid subscription. Blocks automated access. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [eInnsyn](https://einnsyn.no/) — common search of public mail journals (postjournaler) of state agencies, Oslo municipality and other public bodies; access requests for documents can be sent anonymously and free of charge. Personal names stop being searchable after 12 months. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [Norges domstoler (Norwegian Courts)](https://www.domstol.no/) — courts' portal: schedules of court hearings, court contact details and information for parties. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [Brønnøysundregistrene — Kunngjøringer (Register Announcements)](https://w2.brreg.no/kunngjoring/) — official announcements from the Brønnøysund Register Centre: new registrations, changes, bankruptcies, liquidations and forced dissolutions; search by organisation number, name or date. *Lang: NB.*
- [NewsWeb (Oslo Børs)](https://newsweb.oslobors.no/) — Euronext Oslo's service for regulatory announcements of listed issuers: annual and interim reports, insider trades and major shareholdings. *Lang: NB, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [Enhetsregisteret — Virksomhetsopplysninger (Central Coordinating Register for Legal Entities)](https://virksomhet.brreg.no/nb/oppslag/enheter) — Brønnøysund Register Centre's free search by organisation number or name: address, legal form, roles (board, CEO, auditor), status and registry history; filters by industry and municipality. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Aksjonærregisteret (Shareholder Register)](https://www.skatteetaten.no/deling/aksjonarregisteret/) — Tax Administration register of shareholders in all Norwegian limited companies and foreign companies listed on Oslo Børs, as of 31 December for each income year; anyone can get access. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [Finanstilsynet — Virksomhetsregister (FSA Register of Entities)](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/virksomhetsregisteret/) — register of financial institutions, investment firms, real-estate agents, auditors and other entities licensed or registered by the Financial Supervisory Authority. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Proff.no](https://www.proff.no/) — company search with financial figures, official roles, shareholders and addresses for all companies that file annual accounts with the Brønnøysund Register Centre. *Lang: NB.*
- [Regnskapstall.no](https://www.regnskapstall.no/) — company search by Opplysningen 1881: accounts, roles and role networks, new registrations and bankruptcies; extended paid version with pledges and ratings. *Lang: NB.*
- [North Data](https://www.northdata.com/) — North Data covers company data in Norway as part of its XL package, which includes comprehensive information on companies, key persons, and financials. *Lang: EN, DE, FR.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Norgeskart](https://www.norgeskart.no/) — Norwegian Mapping Authority's national map: topographic, aerial and nautical layers, property boundaries and place names; search by address or cadastral number. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- [Eiendomsregisteret (Kartverket Property Register)](https://eiendomsregisteret.kartverket.no/) — Norwegian Mapping Authority service for property information by address or cadastral number (gnr./bnr.), based on the land register (grunnbok) and cadastre (matrikkel); check current terms for owner and mortgage data. *Lang: NB.*
- [Norge i bilder](https://www.norgeibilder.no/) — orthophotos of all of Norway, including historical aerial photos for comparing changes over time. *Lang: NB.*
- [Geonorge](https://www.geonorge.no/) — national catalogue for finding, viewing and downloading public map data. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Kommunekart](https://www.kommunekart.com/) — map portal used by many municipalities: zoning plans, property boundaries, buildings and municipal map layers. *Lang: NB.*
- [Hjemla](https://www.hjemla.no/) — property data for all 2.5 million homes in Norway: sold prices, listings and neighbourhood information; search by address or Finn link. *Lang: NB.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Statens vegvesen — Kjøretøyopplysninger (Vehicle Information)](https://www.vegvesen.no/kjoretoy/kjop-og-salg/kjoretoyopplysninger/) — Public Roads Administration's vehicle lookup: EU inspection deadline, engine, weight, tyres and emissions without login; the owner can be found by logging in to Din side or by SMS. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [1881 — Søk bil og vis eier (Vehicle Owner Search)](https://www.1881.no/) — directory service with a vehicle search by registration number that shows the owner. *Lang: NB.*

### Maritime
- [Skipssøk (Norwegian Maritime Authority Ship Search)](https://skipssok.sdir.no/) — search of the Norwegian ship registers, the Norwegian Ordinary Ship Register (NOR) and the Norwegian International Ship Register (NIS); needs JavaScript. *Lang: NB.*
- [NAIS (Norwegian Coastal Administration)](https://nais.kystverket.no/) — live AIS map of vessels in Norwegian waters. *Lang: NB.*
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [Norges luftfartøyregister — Registrerte luftfartøy med eiere (Norwegian Aircraft Register Open Data)](https://data.norge.no/nb/datasets/ca241ae5-fc9e-3702-bbcd-5453d2d0f06f/registrerte-luftfartoy-med-eiere) — Civil Aviation Authority open dataset (JSON, updated daily) of all LN- registered aircraft with type, manufacturer, owner or contact and registration date; not every register field is included. *Lang: NB.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Skatteetaten — Søk i skattelistene (Tax Lists Search)](https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/forms/search-the-tax-lists/) — income, wealth and tax of individuals and businesses (currently income year 2024; lists for 2025 published from 2 Dec 2026). Requires ID-porten login, up to 500 viewed persons per month; searches are logged and visible to the person searched. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Opplysningen 1881](https://www.1881.no/) — telephone directory: search persons and companies by name, number or address; also property prices and a "who called" app. *Lang: NB.*
- [Stortinget — Verv og økonomiske interesser (Parliament — Register of Positions and Financial Interests)](https://www.stortinget.no/no/stortinget-og-demokratiet/representantene/okonomiske-interesser/) — mandatory register of MPs' and government members' board seats, jobs, shareholdings and other interests, updated regularly as PDF. *Lang: NB.*
- [Stortinget — Biografier (Parliament — Biographies)](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Representanter-og-komiteer/Representantene/Biografier/) — biographies of current and former MPs. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Valgresultat.no (Election Results)](https://www.valgresultat.no/) — official results of parliamentary, municipal and county elections by party, municipality and polling district. *Lang: NB.*
- [Helsepersonellregisteret — HPR (Health Personnel Register)](https://register.helsedirektoratet.no/hpr) — public register of all authorisations and licences of health personnel and veterinarians, with history and administrative sanctions; requires ID-porten login. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Advokaten hjelper deg — Finn advokat (Norwegian Bar Association — Find a Lawyer)](https://advokatenhjelperdeg.no/) — Bar Association's search of member lawyers by county. *Lang: NB.*
- [Digitalarkivet (Digital Archives)](https://www.digitalarkivet.no/) — National Archives' free service: person search, censuses, church books, probate records and land registration sources. *Lang: NB, NN, EN.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [Doffin](https://www.doffin.no/) — national database for public procurement notices and contract awards. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [KOFA — Klagenemnda for offentlige anskaffelser (Complaints Board for Public Procurement)](https://www.klagenemndssekretariatet.no/klagenemda-for-offentlige-anskaffelser-kofa) — complaints board decisions on disputed public tenders. *Lang: NB.*
- [Anskaffelser.no (DFØ Procurement Guidance)](https://www.anskaffelser.no/) — Norwegian Agency for Public and Financial Management's guidance and rules on public procurement. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU/EEA procurement-notice search; filter by country Norway for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*
- [Proff Forvalt](https://www.forvalt.no/) — commercial company database with credit checks, ownership structures, roles and tender alerts. Paid, with a free trial. *Lang: NB.*

## WHOIS
- [Norid — Who holds the domain name?](https://www.norid.no/en/domeneoppslag/hvem-har-domenenavnet/) — official .no registry lookup of who holds a domain name. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [Norid — Find domain names registered to an organisation number](https://www.norid.no/en/domeneoppslag/finn-domenenavn-registrert-pa-et-organisasjonsnummer/) — list all .no domains held by one organisation number. *Lang: NB, EN.*
- [IANA — .no Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/no.html) — official delegation data for .no: registry operator Norid, contacts and name servers. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Norwegian networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
